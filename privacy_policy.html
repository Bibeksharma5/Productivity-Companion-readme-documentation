1. Introduction

Productivity Companion is an all-in-one productivity application for Windows, built with privacy and accessibility in mind. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you stay in control.

The app works in two distinct ways, and this distinction is the most important thing to understand about your privacy:

  • Locally — many features (notes, links, recordings, conversions, document reading, device information) run entirely on your computer. Your content never leaves your device.
  • Online, on request — a few features (the AI assistant, image generation, translation, online transcription, text to speech, weather, currency, and Wikipedia search) send only the specific input you give them to a third-party service to process your request. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background for these features.

The developers of Productivity Companion do not operate servers that collect, store, or analyze your data, and the app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking technology.

Note This policy covers the Productivity Companion application itself. The Microsoft Store delivers the app and operates under Microsoft's own privacy statement, and each online service used by the app operates under its own privacy policy, which you can review on the service's website.

2. What we do not collect

To be clear about what this app is not doing, here is what we do not collect:

  • No accounts or registration — you never need to create an account, provide a name, email address, or any other personal information to use the app.
  • No telemetry or analytics — the app does not report your usage habits, crashes, or feature statistics to the developers.
  • No advertising — the app contains no ads and no advertising identifiers (such as advertising IDs) are read or shared.
  • No tracking across apps or websites — there are no trackers, cookies, or fingerprinting technologies in the app.
  • No automatic uploads — your notes, links, documents, recordings, and settings stay on your device unless you explicitly use an online feature with that content.
  • No background monitoring — the app does not monitor your activity, location, or other applications.
Good to know The only automatic network activity is a lightweight update check on startup (when enabled) that asks the project's version page whether a newer version exists. It sends no personal data — only a standard request, like visiting a webpage.

The following information is stored only on your computer, inside the app's private data folder. It is never transmitted anywhere by the app.

Where this data lives

On Windows, the app stores its data in a private folder inside your user profile, for example:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\BlindTechNexus.ProductivityCompanion_bpastbqzbm4dw\LocalState

Temporary files use the sibling TempState folder. Because these folders sit inside your Windows user profile, they are protected by your Windows sign-in, just like your Documents folder.

Note Content you create locally — such as notes and links — is not encrypted by the app itself. As with any file on your computer, it is protected by the security of your Windows user account. Keep your account password and device secure.

5. Microphone, camera & file access

The app only uses your hardware and files when you explicitly ask it to, and only for the task at hand.

  • Microphone — used only by the Voice Recorder while you are actively recording. The recording is saved as a file on your computer. The microphone is never used in the background or without your action.
  • Camera — the Image Describer can take a picture with your webcam when you choose "Take picture". The photo is captured only for that purpose and stays on your device unless you ask the AI to describe it.
  • File access — the app only opens files you select yourself (documents, images, audio, video). It does not scan, index, or upload your files, and it requests no broad file-system permissions.
  • Documents — the Document Reader processes the file you open on your device. It is sent online only if you explicitly use an online feature on it (Ask AI assistant, Deep inside with AI, translation, or online speech).

6. How your data is used

  • Local data is used solely to make the features work: to save and retrieve your notes and links, to remember your preferences, and to power conversions and recordings.
  • Online data is used solely to fulfil the specific request you make (translate this text, describe this image, transcribe this audio, and so on).
  • Feedback is read by the developers to improve the application and to respond to you.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or traded, and it is never used for advertising.

The developers do not have access to your local data, and they do not operate any service that receives it. Everything local stays local.

7. Retention & deletion

Local data

Your local data stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual notes or links inside the Notes Manager and Link Saver.
  • Delete downloaded models, caches, and settings with Settings → More → Clear productivity companion cache.
  • Uninstall the application to remove the app itself (the Store uninstaller will remove the app's files; content you created is stored in your profile, so you may also want to delete the app's data folder manually if you wish to remove it completely).

Online data

  • Files uploaded to the AI service (documents, audio) are deleted after your request is processed, and the app cleans them up automatically.
  • Conversation history in the AI Assistant is kept in memory only while the window is open; it is not written to your disk by the app, and it disappears when you close the window.
  • Other online services may retain the content you send them for their own operational purposes, according to their own privacy policies, which you can review on each service's website.
Tip If you care about privacy above all, choose the offline options: download the Whisper model for offline transcription and Tesseract for offline OCR once, and most of your daily work never touches the internet at all.

8. Security

  • Encrypted connections — all network communication uses HTTPS, so data in transit is encrypted.
  • No passwords to protect — because the app has no accounts, there are no account credentials for anyone to steal.
  • Minimal data — the app collects the smallest amount of information needed to work, which reduces risk by design.
  • Your responsibility — local files are protected by your Windows user account. Use a strong sign-in, keep your system updated, and be mindful when uploading sensitive documents to online features.

If you believe you have found a security issue in the application, please report it through the in-app feedback form so the developers can address it promptly.

9. Children's privacy

Productivity Companion is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it does not require any personal information to use. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the feedback form, please contact the developers (see section 11) and it will be removed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the application evolves — for example, when a feature is added, changed, or removed. When we do:

  • The Last updated and Effective date at the top of this page are revised.
  • The updated policy is included with the next app update, and this page is updated in the app's documentation.

Your continued use of the application after an update means you accept the revised policy. We encourage you to check this page from time to time (open Help from the home screen, or press F1).

11. Contact us

We are happy to answer any questions you have about this policy or about your data. The easiest ways to reach us:

  • In-app feedback — press Send feedback (Alt+K) on the home screen. Your name, optional email, and message go directly to the developers.
