1. Introduction

Welcome to Productivity Companion — a rich desktop application that brings 31 everyday productivity tools together into a clean, modern, and accessible window. Whether you want to read an e-book aloud with natural speech, edit media using over 40 editing operations, share your screen while having a live voice conversation with Gemini AI, read the latest curated technology news, convert complex documents between formats, scan or generate QR codes, transcribe recordings, or organize your notes, Productivity Companion has a dedicated tool ready for you.

The application is engineered from the ground up with accessibility as a first-class feature. It works seamlessly with full keyboard navigation and popular Windows screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, and Narrator. Controls are clearly labelled, dialogs announce their results, focus is never lost, embedded web viewers are forced into Chromium's accessibility tree, and every single feature can be comfortably used without touching a mouse.

What can you do with it?

  • Read and deeply analyze documents — PDF, EPUB, Word (DOCX), and PowerPoint (PPTX) files with plain text and formatted views, TTS, OCR, automatic background page prefetching, and AI analysis that renders equations in accessible MathML.
  • Unified document conversion — convert text to PDF, Word to PDF, Word to EPUB, HTML to Markdown, and Markdown to HTML.
  • Conversational & live AI — chat with an AI assistant with web search, thinking mode, file attachments, and complete chat history management; have real-time duplex voice conversations in Live Assistant with camera vision and desktop screen sharing; get scene-by-scene video descriptions; and generate AI images.
  • Curated tech articles — browse the latest technology news and articles with markdown formatting, syntax-highlighted code snippets with copy buttons, offline caching, and accessible embedded web viewing.
  • Comprehensive audio & video editing — a redesigned editor featuring over 40 specialized tools: multi-clip timeline, trimming with hotkeys, cutting, splitting, merging with crossfade, 5-band parametric equalizer, compressor/limiter, noise reduction & AI denoise, background music with auto-ducking, text and image watermarks, picture-in-picture, chroma key, subtitles, transitions, EBU R128 loudness normalization, LUT color grading, video stabilization, and animated GIF creation.
  • Audio & screen recording — record your display, microphone, and soundcard (WASAPI loopback) into video or audio files with independent microphone and soundcard volume adjustments (0%–200%), or record voice memos with pause, resume, and segment continuation.
  • QR code scanning & generation — scan QR codes from files or camera in seconds, and generate crisp QR code images from any link or text.
  • Stay organized & informed — manage categorized notes and web links with JSON backups, stay notified with the Notification Center, convert currencies with live exchange rates, check detailed weather forecasts, and inspect comprehensive hardware, power, and battery specifications.

Internet vs. offline

Tools in this guide are marked with an Online badge when they require an active internet connection, or an Offline badge when they run locally on your PC. Tools marked with a dagger (†) run completely offline after a one-time, in-app download of their engine or language model (such as Tesseract OCR, Pandoc, full FFmpeg modules, or Whisper models).

2. Getting started

Installation

Productivity Companion is distributed safely and conveniently through the Microsoft Store:

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on your Windows PC.
  2. Search for Productivity Companion.
  3. Select the app and click Get (or Install) to download and install it.
  4. Launch Productivity Companion from the Start menu, desktop shortcut, or directly from the Microsoft Store.

Your first launch

When the application opens for the first time, a Welcome dialog greets you. It introduces the latest features in Version 26.4 and offers two buttons:

  • What's new (Alt+N) — opens the detailed release notes.
  • Got it (Enter) — closes the dialog and takes you straight to the home screen.

If you prefer not to see this dialog every time the app opens, check the Don't show this dialog again box before closing it.

Automatic update checks On startup, Productivity Companion quietly verifies if a newer version is available in the Microsoft Store. If an update is found, a prompt lets you open the Store page directly to install it. If you are already running the latest version, no intrusive dialog is displayed. You can toggle automatic update checks in Settings → General.

3. The home screen

The home screen provides a centralized, clutter-free hub for all 31 built-in tools. The instructions are clearly announced at the top:

"Select feature and press enter to open (right click to see more options)"

Opening a feature

  1. Use the Up and Down arrow keys to navigate through the list of tools.
  2. Press Enter (or double-click with the mouse) to open the selected feature.

When a feature window opens, the home screen neatly minimizes or hides. When you close the feature or press its Back button (Alt+B, Esc, or Alt+F4), the home screen immediately restores focus right where you left off.

Reordering features

Customize the list so your favorite or most frequent tools appear right at the top:

  1. Highlight a tool in the list.
  2. Press the Application key, Shift+F10, or right-click to open the context menu.
  3. Choose one of the reordering actions:
    • Open feature — opens the selected tool.
    • Move up — shifts the tool up one position in the list.
    • Move down — shifts the tool down one position in the list.
    • Move top — immediately jumps the tool to the very top.
    • Move bottom — immediately jumps the tool to the very bottom.

Your custom order is automatically saved across sessions in your application data directory. To return to standard alphabetical order, simply activate the Reset features order button (Alt+R).

Home screen layout & navigation

The home screen interface provides a clean, streamlined top-and-bottom layout:

…More options (Alt+M)

Opens a popup menu containing Settings, Notification center, Help and privacy, What's new, Send feedback, and Open app in Microsoft Store.

Help (Direct Key) (F1)

Pressing F1 anywhere on the home screen immediately opens this comprehensive documentation in your default web browser.

Reset features order (Alt+R)

Re-sorts the entire feature list back into standard alphabetical sorting.

Exit (Alt+E)

Safely closes the Productivity Companion application.

The More Options menu

Clicking …More options or pressing Alt+M presents the following actions:

  • Settings (Alt+I) — open the comprehensive settings notebook.
  • Notification center (Alt+T) — open the notification center to view important updates, announcements, and news from developers.
  • Help and privacy (Alt+H) — open the Help & Privacy dialog to view documentation, privacy statement, version details, and update checks.
  • What's new (Alt+N) — view the latest release notes and new feature summaries.
  • Send feedback (Alt+K) — send direct suggestions, bug reports, or translation inquiries to the development team.
  • Open app in the Microsoft Store — jump directly to the app's product page in the Windows Store.

4. Settings

Open Settings from the …More options menu (Alt+M → Settings or Alt+I) to configure themes, font sizing, display languages, voice engines, offline models, recording volume levels, and default paths. The settings window is arranged in scrollable notebook tabs. Use Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab to move between tabs. Press OK (Enter) to save changes or Cancel (Esc) to discard.

1. General

  • Application theme — select Light, Dark, or System default. The theme applies instantly across all windows while preserving native control roles for screen readers.
  • Application font size — choose Normal, Large, or Extra Large to scale fonts across all dialogs and screens for optimal visual comfort.
  • Program display language — choose among English, नेपाली (Nepali), Türkçe (Turkish), Tiếng Việt (Vietnamese), Français (French), हिन्दी (Hindi), or தமிழ் (Tamil). Changing language or font size prompts you to restart the app.
  • Automatically check for updates on startup — toggle automatic background update notifications.

2. Text to speech

  • TTS engine — choose among three powerful engines:
    • Google text to speech — fast, multilingual online synthesis with a wide choice of languages.
    • Microsoft edge voices — high-fidelity neural voices. Select your favorite voice, adjust speed (-100% to +100%), pitch (-100Hz to +100Hz), and volume (-100% to +100%), or click Fetch latest voices to refresh available voices.
    • Gemini AI — 30 expressive neural voices powered by Gemini (including Zephyr, Puck, Charon, Kore, Fenrir, Leda, Orus, Aoede, Callirrhoe, and more) with natural pacing and personality descriptions.

3. Audio transcription

  • Transcription mode — choose Online (cloud-based, instant, zero setup) or Offline (private, runs locally on your PC).
  • Whisper model — for offline mode, download free faster-whisper models: tiny, tiny.en, base, base.en, small, small.en, medium, medium.en, large-v3-turbo, distil-large-v3, distil-large-v2, large-v3, large-v2, large-v1. The button updates automatically to show Download or Delete for the selected model.

4. Recorder

  • Default recording directory — folder where voice recordings are saved.
  • Channel — Mono or Stereo.
  • Sampling rate — 44100 Hz or 48000 Hz.
  • Record as — WAV (uncompressed) or MP3 (compressed).
  • Microphone volume (%) — adjust microphone recording input gain from 0% to 200% (default 100%).

5. Screen recorder

  • Default screen recording directory — folder where screen captures and mixed recordings are saved.
  • Record as — choose Video or Audio default mode.
  • Select format — choose output container: video (mp4, mkv, avi, mov, webm, wmv) or audio (mp3, wav, aac, m4a, flac, ogg).
  • Sound card volume (%) — adjust system playback loopback recording volume from 0% to 200% (default 100%).
  • Microphone volume (%) — adjust microphone input volume during screen recording from 0% to 200% (default 100%).

6. Conversion

Manages the Pandoc document conversion engine used by the Document Converter for Word to EPUB, HTML to Markdown, and Markdown to HTML. Click Download Pandoc to install or Remove Pandoc to uninstall.

7. OCR

Manages the Tesseract OCR engine used by Image OCR and Document Reader OCR:

  • Download / Remove Tesseract OCR — install or remove the core OCR binary.
  • Download additional languages for OCR — browse all languages from the official repository, select Fast model (lightweight) or Best model (maximum precision), monitor download progress, or delete installed languages.

8. Document reader

  • Zoom % (10–500%) and Font size (6–48).
  • High contrast and Invert colors visual options.
  • Auto-scroll speed (0 = slow, 9 = fast).
  • Extraction profile (default, simple, detailed, ocr_only, clean).
  • Default export directory and Enable TTS chunking.
  • Automatically analyze the next page in deep inside web viewer — when enabled, the reader silently pre-analyzes the next page in the background while you read the current page, ensuring instantaneous page turns in the Deep Inside viewer.

9. More

  • Clear productivity companion cache — safely cleans temporary files, cached voices, downloaded Whisper models, and custom feature orders when troubleshooting. Notes and links are preserved safely.

6. AI & knowledge tools

6.1 AI Assistant Online

A conversational assistant built into Productivity Companion. Ask questions, brainstorm, summarize complex documents, solve math problems, or attach files. The assistant features intelligent key rotation, web search integration, thinking mode, dynamic window title updates matching the user's initial question, and complete Chat History management.

How to use it

  1. Open AI Assistant from the home screen. The home view presents New conversation and Chat history.
  2. Click New conversation to start a fresh chat session.
  3. Optionally check Web search to ground answers in real-time online data, or Thinking for deep multi-step reasoning.
  4. Use Attach file to attach any text, PDF, image, audio, or video file directly with your question.
  5. Type your question and press Enter or click Send. The button toggles to Stop response during generation.
  6. Use the context menu (Shift+F10 or Application key) on any message to:
    • Open — view message text in an accessible reader window.
    • View formatted — view rendered HTML with MathML equations.
    • Listen / Generate audio — listen immediately using default TTS or export to MP3.
    • Copy / Save as — copy to clipboard or save as Markdown/Text.
    • Translate — send the response straight to Text Translator.
    • View thoughts — inspect the assistant's internal reasoning chain if Thinking was enabled.

Managing Chat History

Click Chat history (Alt+H) on the AI Assistant home screen to manage your saved chats:

  • Browse and resume: Navigate through past conversations displaying title and last opened date, then press Enter or double-click to continue chatting.
  • Context actions: Right-click or press Shift+F10 on any conversation to Open chat or Delete chat.
  • Export history: Save your complete chat history to a JSON backup file for archiving or transfer.
  • Import history: Restore previous chat histories from a JSON file.
  • Clear history: Safely clear all chat sessions with confirmation.

6.2 Live Assistant Online

Experience real-time, low-latency conversational voice AI powered by Gemini. Live Assistant enables natural, two-way voice conversations with audio streaming, optional live camera vision, and desktop screen sharing.

How to use it

  1. Open Live Assistant from the home screen.
  2. Click Start conversation (Alt+S) to begin the live audio stream.
  3. Speak naturally into your microphone — the assistant streams its spoken answer back in real time.
  4. Toggle Turn on microphone (Alt+M) to mute or unmute during the call.
  5. Toggle Turn on camera (Alt+C) to stream real-time frames from your webcam, allowing Gemini to see objects, documents, or surroundings and describe them to you.
  6. Toggle Share screen (Alt+S) to stream real-time frames of your computer display with cursor tracking. Gemini can see your active desktop, open windows, applications, code, websites, and documents and help you troubleshoot or explain what is on screen.
  7. Click Report inappropriate response (Alt+R) while a live conversation is running if the assistant generates problematic, harmful, or inappropriate content to submit a report for review.
  8. Click Stop conversation (Alt+S) or Back to end the session.
Camera and screen share switching Camera streaming and screen sharing are mutually exclusive. When screen sharing is checked, the webcam automatically pauses; checking camera turns off screen sharing. When both are unchecked, Gemini interacts purely via voice.

6.3 Tech Articles Online / Offline (Cached)

Stay informed with curated technology news, tutorials, and articles designed for screen reader accessibility and tech enthusiasts. Articles are retrieved from a cloud storage service and cached locally so you can read previously opened articles even without an active internet connection.

How to use it

  1. Open Tech articles from the home screen.
  2. Browse the list of available articles sorted in descending chronological order (latest published first). The list displays Title, Author, and Published Date.
  3. Press Enter (or double-click) on an article to open it in the built-in accessible web viewer.
  4. In the article reader, read rich semantic content with clean typography, styled headings, lists, blockquotes, and pre-formatted code blocks equipped with one-click Copy buttons. External links open directly in your default web browser.
  5. Press Esc or click Close to return to the articles list.
  6. Use the context menu (Shift+F10 or right-click) on any article in the list to:
    • Read article — open the full article in the reader.
    • Refresh articles — fetch the latest publications from the server (F5).
    • Copy article title — copy the headline to your clipboard.
    • Back to home — return to the application home screen (Esc).
Accessible HTML5 rendering The Tech Articles viewer uses Chromium's forced accessibility tree, ensuring complete heading navigation, link jumping, and continuous reading in JAWS, NVDA, and Narrator without silence or control trapping.

6.4 Wikipedia Search Online

Search Wikipedia across languages, read article summaries, fetch random articles, and export or listen to content.

  1. Open Wikipedia Search.
  2. Select your preferred search language from the language drop-down.
  3. Type a search query and press Enter, or click Random article to discover something new.
  4. Browse matching search results. Use the Quick actions group to open the summary or view the full article in your browser.
  5. From any article summary, use dedicated buttons to Listen via TTS, Generate audio, Copy, or Save to file.

6.5 Text Translator Online

Translate text between a broad selection of global languages with automatic source language detection.

  1. Open Text Translator.
  2. Type or paste your text, or click Load to open a .txt file.
  3. Choose your target language.
  4. Press Translate (Enter). You can cancel at any time while translating.
  5. Read or copy the translated result, or send it to Text To Speech for audio playback.

7. Reading & document tools

7.1 Document Reader Offline*

A powerhouse accessible document workstation supporting PDF, EPUB, Word (DOCX), and PowerPoint (PPTX) files. The reader dynamically updates the window title to the active document name, provides section-by-section navigation, bookmarks, annotations, OCR, and AI-driven deep analysis.

Reading & navigation

  • Open document — Ctrl+I to open PDF, EPUB, DOCX, or PPTX. Enter passwords for protected PDFs.
  • Section navigation — Alt+PageDown for next section/page, Alt+PageUp for previous, and Ctrl+G to jump to a specific section.
  • Reading modes — switch between Plain Text (optimized for screen readers) and Formatted (visual layout).
  • Visual controls — Zoom in (Ctrl++), Zoom out (Ctrl+-), Reset (Ctrl+0), High contrast (Ctrl+H), Invert colors (Ctrl+Shift+I), and Split view (Ctrl+Shift+V).
  • Search — Find (Ctrl+F), next match (F3), previous match (Shift+F3).

AI integration & background prefetching

  • Ask AI assistant (Alt+K) — opens AI Assistant with your active document automatically attached, ready for in-depth questions, section explanations, or summaries.
  • Deep inside with AI (Alt+I, PDF only) — transforms complex PDF pages into pristine accessible presentations where mathematical equations and formulas are converted to MathML for flawless screen reader announcement, charts and tables become structured data, and images receive descriptive alt text.
  • Automatic background page prefetching: While you read in the Deep Inside web viewer, the reader automatically analyzes the next page in the background. When you advance to the next page, the content loads instantly without waiting for AI processing. This can be toggled in Settings → Document reader.
  • Open in web viewer (Alt+W, EPUB/PPTX) — launches a smart formatted web view retaining original styling and structure.

Cache management

Manage cached OCR and Deep Inside analysis data via the Tools menu:

  • Delete OCR cache: Clear OCR Cache for Current Page, Clear OCR Cache for This Document, or Clear All OCR Cache.
  • Delete Deep Inside cache: Delete Deep Inside Cache for Current Page, Delete Deep Inside Cache for This Document, or Delete All Deep Inside Cache.

Speech, translation & OCR

  • Read aloud — Ctrl+R reads the active section with your configured TTS voice.
  • Translate section — Ctrl+Shift+T translates the active section online.
  • OCR section — Ctrl+Shift+O runs Tesseract OCR on scanned/image-based pages. Results are cached locally so repeated visits load instantly.

Study, export & PDF manipulation

  • Bookmarks & Highlights — Bookmark (Ctrl+B), Bookmark manager (Ctrl+Shift+B), Annotations (Ctrl+Shift+N), Highlight selection (Ctrl+Shift+L).
  • Export — Ctrl+Shift+E exports the document as Plain Text, DOCX, Audio book, CSV, JSON, or HTML.
  • PDF Tools — Password protect, unlock, redact sensitive text, rotate pages, split PDF, delete sections, add watermarks, and extract images.

7.2 Document Converter Offline†

A unified, accessible conversion center providing 5 essential document converters in one place:

  1. Open Document Converter from the home screen.
  2. Select the desired conversion module and press Enter:
    • Text to PDF converter — converts plain text or log files (.txt, .log) into styled PDF documents.
    • Word to PDF converter — converts Microsoft Word documents (.docx) into clean PDFs while preserving fonts, tables, alignment, and OMML/MathML math equations.
    • Word to EPub converter — converts Word documents (.docx) into EPUB e-books using Pandoc.
    • HTML to Markdown converter — converts web pages (.html) into Markdown files (.md) using Pandoc.
    • Markdown to HTML converter — converts Markdown files (.md) into styled HTML web pages using Pandoc.
  3. Open your input file, select the destination path, and click Save/Convert. A progress dialog confirms successful completion.

7.3 Image OCR Offline†

Extract text from image files (.png, .jpg, .bmp, etc.) using Tesseract OCR.

  1. Open Image OCR. (If Tesseract is not yet installed, click Yes on the prompt to download it).
  2. Click Open file and pick an image.
  3. Choose the primary recognition language, and optionally configure a secondary language.
  4. Click Start to run OCR.
  5. From the result box, you can Save as text file, Copy, Translate, or Convert to audio.

7.4 PDF to Image Offline

Render every page of a PDF document into high-resolution 300 DPI PNG images.

  1. Open PDF to Image and choose a .pdf file.
  2. Click Export to images and select the target folder.
  3. Pages are rendered and saved as page_1.png, page_2.png, etc.

7.5 Image to PDF Offline

Combine multiple images into a single, organized PDF document.

  1. Open Image to PDF.
  2. Add images to the list. Use context menu options (Move up, Move down, Move top, Move bottom, Remove) to sequence your pages.
  3. Click export, choose a file name, and save your PDF.

8. Audio & video tools

8.1 Text To Speech converter Online

Convert written text into natural-sounding audio files (.mp3) with rich voice selection.

  1. Open Text To Speech converter.
  2. Type your text or load a text file.
  3. The Voice button indicates the current engine: Google TTS, Microsoft Edge voices, or Gemini AI. Press the button or visit Settings → Text to speech to switch engines and customize voices.
  4. Click Generate, choose your output location, and save your MP3.
  5. Use Play to listen back directly within the integrated player.

8.2 Audio Transcriber Online / Offline

Transcribe spoken words from audio files (.mp3, .wav, .flac, .ogg, .m4a) into clean text.

  1. Open Audio Transcriber.
  2. Configure your mode in Settings → Audio transcription: Online (cloud AI) or Offline (Whisper models downloaded to your PC).
  3. Click Select audio file to transcribe and choose your recording.
  4. Transcription runs with progress feedback and speaker-change detection markers ([speaker changed]).
  5. Copy the transcript, translate it, or generate new audio from the output.

8.3 Voice Recorder Offline

Record microphone audio directly with pause, resume, segment continuation, deletion, and in-app playback.

  1. Open Voice Recorder.
  2. Adjust recording options in Settings → Recorder: choose format (WAV/MP3), channels (mono/stereo), sampling rate (44100/48000 Hz), and Microphone volume (0% to 200%).
  3. Click Start recording (Alt+S).
  4. Use Pause recording / Resume recording (Alt+P) as needed.
  5. Click Stop recording to save the file.
  6. After stopping, use Play to review, Continue recording to append new audio to the file, or Delete to discard.

8.4 Screen Recorder Offline

A full-featured screen and audio recorder capable of recording your computer display, microphone, and soundcard (WASAPI loopback system audio) into video or audio formats with independent volume controls.

  1. Open Screen recorder from the home screen.
  2. Select the recording sources by checking:
    • Record display — captures full desktop video and mouse cursor.
    • Record microphone — captures your voice from the default mic.
    • Record soundcard — captures Windows system audio (games, videos, meeting audio, music) via low-latency WASAPI loopback.
  3. Adjust volume levels in Settings → Screen recorder: set Sound card volume (%) (0%–200%) and Microphone volume (%) (0%–200%) for optimal audio balance.
  4. Choose your output directory and format (Video: mp4, mkv, avi, etc. / Audio: mp3, wav, m4a, etc.).
  5. Click Start recording (Alt+R).
  6. Use Pause recording (Alt+P) to pause/resume.
  7. Click Stop recording (Alt+R). Your video and audio streams are muxed seamlessly with FFmpeg.
  8. Review with Play recording, append more footage with Continue recording, or remove with Delete recording.

8.5 Video to Audio converter Offline

Extract audio tracks from video files with single or batch conversion.

  1. Open Video to audio converter.
  2. Click Open Video file (or check Enable multiple video conversion to load up to 10 videos).
  3. Select the output audio format: mp3, wav, or m4a.
  4. Optionally check Enable high quality conversion, then click Save to audio.

8.6 Audio to Video converter Offline

Transform audio tracks into video files (.mp4, .mkv) with custom artwork for social platforms.

  1. Open Audio to video converter and select your audio file.
  2. Optionally click Add Image to Video to choose cover art.
  3. Select your aspect ratio: 16:9 (horizontal) or 9:16 (vertical reels/shorts).
  4. Click Convert to render the video.

8.7 Audio Video Editor Offline†

A completely redesigned, powerhouse media editing suite featuring over 40 specialized editing tools. Every tool is keyboard-accessible, announces progress clearly to screen readers, and previews results in the integrated media player.

12. Accessibility

Productivity Companion is engineered specifically to empower users of all visual, auditory, and motor abilities:

  • Screen reader compatibility — fully compatible with NVDA, JAWS, and Windows Narrator. Accessible names, roles, states, and live announcements are exposed continuously.
  • Embedded web viewer accessibility with JAWS — on Windows, embedded web views (used in Deep Inside reading and Tech Articles) use the Microsoft Edge WebView2 backend. Productivity Companion forces Chromium's renderer accessibility tree on via --force-renderer-accessibility at startup, enabling JAWS to read, navigate by headings, and interact with web views just like standard web browsers.
  • Native checkbox and radio button style preservation — in Windows dark theme, controls maintain their native accessibility styles so JAWS reliably announces them as real checkboxes/radio buttons and reports checked/unchecked status instead of treating them as generic buttons.
  • Full keyboard independence — every workflow can be accomplished using standard keyboard shortcuts, arrow keys, and mnemonic access keys without mouse intervention. Pressing Esc reliably navigates back to previous screens.
  • Dynamic typography scaling — choose between Normal, Large, and Extra Large font sizes in Settings → General to optimize legibility across all dialogs.
  • High contrast & dark mode — seamless adaptation to system dark mode with manual theme overrides, high-contrast toggles, and inverted color modes inside reading tools.
  • MathML mathematical rendering — equations in PDFs and AI Assistant responses are rendered with proper MathML semantics so screen readers announce equations accurately instead of corrupt text.
  • Multilingual interface — fully translated into English, Nepali, Turkish, Vietnamese, French, Hindi, and Tamil.

13. Tips & troubleshooting

Common questions & solutions

Troubleshooting guide
SituationResolution
"The audio video editor tool requires FFmpeg modules to work" Click Download now when prompted. The comprehensive FFmpeg build will be downloaded and extracted into your local application state folder.
"Pandoc is required for conversion" Click Yes when prompted or open Settings → Conversion → Download Pandoc.
"Tesseract OCR is not downloaded" Click Yes on the prompt or go to Settings → OCR → Download Tesseract OCR.
OCR language missing Go to Settings → OCR → Download additional languages for OCR and install the language (Fast or Best model).
Offline transcription model not found Open Settings → Audio transcription, select a Whisper model (e.g. base or large-v3-turbo), click Download, and switch the mode to Offline.
Webcam not detected in Live Assistant or QR Scanner Ensure your camera is connected and Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera allows desktop app access.
Soundcard loopback audio is silent Verify your Windows default output playback device is active and not muted in Windows Volume Mixer, and verify Soundcard volume in Settings → Screen recorder is set to at least 100%.
Live Assistant screen sharing shows black screen Ensure your display adapter driver is updated and desktop composition is enabled. Screen sharing automatically tracks your active monitor and cursor.
Clearing Deep Inside AI cache In Document Reader, open the Tools menu → Delete deep inside cache to clear cache for the current page, the open document, or all documents.
Offline access to Tech Articles Previously opened articles and post indexes are cached locally in your application directory and remain readable offline. Press Refresh (F5) when connected to update.
Resetting app configuration Open Settings → More → Clear productivity companion cache to restore settings to defaults. (Notes and links are preserved safely).
